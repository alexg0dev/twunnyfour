FAQ

Common questions

Is NGAV enough on its own?

For smaller environments with limited attack surface, Prevent is a strong baseline. Organizations with remote work, privileged access, or compliance requirements usually add Insight EDR for visibility and faster containment.

When do we need MDR?

Choose Complete MDR when you lack 24/7 staffing, want guaranteed response SLAs, or need experts to own remediation rather than only alerting your team.

How does this relate to email security?

Email reduces initial delivery of threats; endpoint security handles what still lands—or arrives via USB, software installs, and browser downloads. Most mature programs run both.
