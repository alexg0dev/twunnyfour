The perimeter

Most breaches still start in the inbox or on a laptop

Attackers do not need exotic zero-days when a convincing invoice, a compromised SaaS token, or an unpatched endpoint will do. TwunnyFour focuses on communication and devices— the two surfaces where trust is decided every day.

We package zero-trust outcomes—verify identity, limit blast radius, inspect content, respond quickly—into products security leaders can deploy without a year-long transformation.

Email is the front door

Phishing, BEC, and spoofed domains remain the cheapest path into finance and admin accounts. Stopping malicious messages early collapses entire classes of incident work.

Endpoints are the foothold

Once malware lands, lateral movement begins. NGAV, EDR, and hardening shrink that window from hours to seconds.

Identity binds both

Stolen credentials turn a single phish into a cloud-wide compromise. MFA and behavioral analytics close the gap between mailbox and infrastructure.
