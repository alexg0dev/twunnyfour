Why it matters

Most breaches still start in the inbox or on a laptop

Attackers do not need exotic zero-days when a convincing invoice, a compromised SaaS token, or an unpatched endpoint will do. TwunnyFour focuses on the two surfaces where business actually happens: communication and devices.

We frame defense as a zero-trust program—verify identity, limit blast radius, inspect content, and respond quickly—then package that into products your team can deploy without a year-long transformation project.

Email is the front door

Phishing, BEC, and spoofed domains remain the cheapest way into finance, HR, and admin accounts. Stopping malicious messages early removes entire classes of downstream incident work.

Endpoints are the foothold

Once malware lands on a laptop or server, lateral movement begins. NGAV, EDR, and hardening shrink that window from hours to seconds.

Identity binds both

Stolen credentials turn a single phish into a cloud-wide compromise. MFA enforcement and behavioral analytics close the gap between mailbox and infrastructure.
