The problem

Email is still where trust gets exploited

A single convincing message can move money, reset MFA, or install malware. Traditional spam filters catch volume; they struggle with targeted social engineering, lookalike domains, and links that only turn dangerous after delivery.

TwunnyFour email security treats every message as untrusted until content, sender reputation, authentication, and user context say otherwise.

Phishing & quishing

AI link inspection and payload analysis catch weaponized URLs—including QR campaigns—before users click.

BEC & spoofing

Contextual banners, brand impersonation checks, and DMARC policy management reduce wire-fraud and domain abuse.

People risk

Simulations and coaching turn the workforce into an early warning layer instead of a permanent weak point.

FAQ

Common questions

Does this replace Microsoft or Google's built-in security?

It complements them. Native controls are a baseline; TwunnyFour adds dedicated AI inspection, anti-BEC context, DMARC operations, and collaboration coverage.

Who should own email security internally?

Typically a security lead partners with messaging admins. Starter plans suit lean IT teams; Advanced and Collaboration modules fit organizations with dedicated SOC or compliance obligations.

Can we deploy only DMARC or awareness first?

Yes. Modules are purchasable independently so you can sequence work—authenticate domains, train users, then deepen gateway controls.
