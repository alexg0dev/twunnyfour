The problem

Email is still where trust gets exploited

A single convincing message can move money, reset MFA, or install malware. Traditional spam filters catch volume; they struggle with targeted social engineering, lookalike domains, and malicious links that only turn dangerous after delivery.

TwunnyFour email security treats every message as untrusted until content, sender reputation, authentication, and user context say otherwise.

Phishing & quishing

AI link inspection and payload analysis catch weaponized URLs—including QR-based campaigns—before users click.

BEC & spoofing

Contextual banners, brand impersonation checks, and DMARC policy management reduce wire-fraud and domain abuse.

People risk

Simulations and coaching turn the workforce into an early warning layer instead of a permanent weak point.

Solution

Layered controls around the mailbox

Start with a cloud gateway, deepen with advanced anti-BEC, authenticate your domains, train users, then extend the same philosophy into chat and file collaboration.

Inspect before trust

Messages are analyzed for malicious URLs, attachments, impersonation patterns, and anomalous sender behavior at the gateway—before they become habits in the inbox.

Authenticate the brand

DMARC Analyzer helps you move from monitoring spoofing to enforcing policy, improving both security and legitimate deliverability.

Coach continuous behavior

Engage Awareness scores risk from simulations and delivers targeted education instead of annual one-size-fits-all training.

Protect the side channels

Collaboration Security covers Teams, Slack, and SharePoint so attackers cannot simply move the conversation off email.

FAQ

Common questions

Does this replace Microsoft or Google’s built-in security?

It complements them. Native controls are a baseline; TwunnyFour adds dedicated AI inspection, anti-BEC context, DMARC operations, and collaboration coverage that most tenants never fully operationalize alone.

Who should own email security internally?

Typically a security lead partners with messaging admins. Starter plans suit lean IT teams; Advanced and Collaboration modules fit organizations with dedicated SOC or compliance obligations.

Can we deploy only DMARC or awareness first?

Yes. Modules are purchasable independently so you can sequence work—authenticate domains, train users, then deepen gateway controls as risk and budget allow.
