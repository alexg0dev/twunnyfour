Pricing

Simple, transparent pricing

Scalable security packages for organizations of every size. Start with a plan, then add email modules, endpoint modules, or individual capabilities as your program matures.

What you are buying

Plans are a baseline, not a cage

Starter establishes email and antivirus coverage for small teams. Business adds AI phishing defense, full EDR, and vulnerability scanning. Enterprise layers managed response and integrated threat intel for organizations that cannot afford slow containment.

Can we mix plans with a-la-carte modules?

Yes. Many customers start on Business, then add Collaboration Security or Falcon OverWatch from the product pages.

How is billing calculated?

Platform plans are per user / month. Email and endpoint modules are priced per the product cards. Capabilities are per node / month.

Is login required to purchase?

Yes. Cart items sync to your TwunnyFour account via Supabase so deployments stay consistent across devices.

Need a custom perimeter?

Mix capabilities from the home map with email and endpoint modules. Volume discussions available for 500+ nodes.
